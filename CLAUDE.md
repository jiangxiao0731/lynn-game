## Design Context

### Users

This is a flagship portfolio demo shown primarily through a recorded showcase. The full playable path should take roughly 8–10 minutes and support a polished 2–3 minute edited portfolio video. It should demonstrate cohesive art direction, environmental storytelling, Godot/C# engineering, interaction design, and a complete gameplay arc.

### Brand Personality

Gentle, melancholic, hopeful. Polluted creatures are victims to purify and restore, never enemies to kill. The emotional arc moves from damage and loneliness toward care, recovery, and renewed ocean life.

### Aesthetic Direction

Use a unified hand-painted underwater storybook style built from the existing painterly assets. Do not generate new assets and do not mix the separate pixel-art libraries into the runtime. Reduce generic neon-glass UI styling; emphasize layered ocean scenery, sediment, drifting life, restrained bioluminescence, and visible polluted-to-restored transitions.

### Design Principles

1. Protection, purification, listening, and restoration are the core verbs.
2. Progress must visibly heal the environment, not only update counters.
3. Reuse stylistically compatible existing assets; procedural art supports motion and feedback.
4. Compose for video: readable objectives, strong zone framing, minimal HUD clutter.
5. Fix sprite slicing, pivots, scale, loops, easing, and all visibly broken animation.
6. Reach 8–10 minutes through varied objectives, discoveries, staged purification, and environmental change—not repetitive grinding.
7. Use no paid or newly generated assets.
